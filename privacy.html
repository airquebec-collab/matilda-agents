Privacy policy — matilda-agents

matilda-agents is a private assistant for its owner and one family member only.

  • Data accessed: Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive of the signed-in user, only to perform tasks for that same user.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own server. Text may be sent to AI model providers (Anthropic via OpenRouter, with no-retention settings) to produce summaries.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train models.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
